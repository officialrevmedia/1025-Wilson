Questions and answers

What buyers and brokers ask most about The Wilson Centre. If your question is not here, call the sales team.

Where is The Wilson Centre?

At 1025 Wilson Street West in Ancaster, at the Wilson and McClure roundabout, with direct access to Highway 403. See the location and drive times.

What sizes are the suites?

Units range from 800 to 17,500 SF. Suites can be combined, so a buyer can take several neighbouring suites or a full floor. See the floor plans.

How much do suites cost?

Suites are priced from the $400,000s. Pricing depends on the suite, its size and its floor. Request the current price list.

How many suites are there?

44 suites across four floors: eight on the ground floor with private street entrances, and twelve on each of Levels 2, 3 and 4, served by an elevator and two stair cores.

What can I use a suite for?

The site is zoned C7, which supports a broad mix of medical, dental, professional office, financial, wellness and ground-floor retail uses. Permitted uses are subject to site-specific zoning exceptions, so confirm your use with the sales team or listing agents before you buy. See zoning and uses.

Can I buy a suite and lease it out?

Yes. You can own to occupy, or hold the suite and lease it to a tenant.

Can I buy more than one suite or a whole floor?

Yes. Suites can be combined to suit your needs, up to a full floor of about 17,500 SF.

Is there an amenity space?

Yes. A landscaped rooftop terrace of over 3,200 SF, served by both stair cores, is shared by owners, staff and clients.

Who is building it?

The Wilson Centre is developed by Elite Developments and designed by WalterFedy.

Do you work with brokers?

Yes. Co-operating opportunities are available for registered brokers and agents. Register as a broker.
