Questions buyers ask first.

All questions and answers

What sizes are the suites?

Units range from 800 to 17,500 SF. Suites can be combined, so a buyer can take several neighbouring suites or a full floor. See the floor plans.

How much do suites cost?

Suites are priced from the $400,000s. Pricing depends on the suite, its size and its floor. Request the current price list.

How many suites are there?

44 suites across four floors: eight on the ground floor with private street entrances, and twelve on each of Levels 2, 3 and 4, served by an elevator and two stair cores.

What can I use a suite for?

The site is zoned C7, which supports a broad mix of medical, dental, professional office, financial, wellness and ground-floor retail uses. Permitted uses are subject to site-specific zoning exceptions, so confirm your use with the sales team or listing agents before you buy. See zoning and uses.
